Privacy policy – Assault Soul: Squad Seven

Last updated: 18 November 2026 (closed test)

1. Controller

The game "Assault Soul: Squad Seven" ("Assault Soul" under its icon, formerly the working title "Outbreak"; the "game") is offered by Salvatore Deniz Bulla, trading as Neonmoor Games, Karl-Wulle-Straße 34, 74076 Heilbronn, Germany ("we", "us"). Email: gfgnp2w@gmail.com, contact form: https://ahnyth.github.io/contact/. We are the controller under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. What data we process, why, and on what legal basis

2.1 Your player account

When the game first starts, our backend provider (Unity Authentication) creates a random player ID for your installation. You do not register: the game asks for no email address and no password. A sign-in token stays on your device so the game recognises you. The game needs a connection to our servers to start and to pay rewards (it is an online game). Purpose and legal basis: providing the game (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, contract).

2.2 Your game progress

Your callsign (the name you choose), class, look, levels, skill points, items and materials, currencies, your safehouse and its crew, the missions you played and their side objectives, and the times of your actions (for daily rewards and the safehouse crew's pay). When a mission starts, our server opens a record of the run (mission, difficulty, squad, start time) and checks the result against it, so rewards cannot be forged. This data lives on our servers, with a copy on your phone. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract).

2.3 Co-op and friends

When you play together, the other players in your squad see your callsign, class, look and level. A co-op mission runs on the players' phones; the phones exchange your button and stick inputs through Unity's relay and session services (Unity Multiplayer Services), which process your IP address to connect the squad. Friends and friend requests store the player IDs and callsigns involved. There is no chat in the game. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (features of the game).

2.4 Crash and error reports

When the game crashes or reports a program error, Unity's diagnostics (Unity Engine Diagnostics) send the error and the place in the program, the device model, the operating system, the app version, the time and a random identifier of the installation. The report holds no callsign and no content of your device. We use it only to find and fix errors. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a working game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); reading the device model and operating system is strictly necessary for this (section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can object at any time (section 6).

2.5 Play statistics, only with your consent

At the first start the game asks whether it may record play statistics. This is voluntary, and the game works the same either way. You can agree only after confirming that you are 16 or older. Only if you agree (or later switch on "Play statistics" in the settings) does the game send to Unity Analytics: device model and operating system, app version, language, session start and length, an identifier of your installation, your player ID, the approximate country that Unity derives from your IP address, and these events: a mission finished (which mission, won or not, difficulty, how long), a co-op mission started (squad size), the tutorial finished. The data is pseudonymous: it holds no name, but the identifiers link it to your installation. We use it to improve the balance. You can withdraw your consent at any time in the settings; the game then sends nothing more and asks Unity to delete what was collected for you. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, section 25(1) TDDDG), withdrawable at any time with effect for the future.

2.6 Technical logs

When the game talks to our servers, our hosting provider processes technical data such as your IP address and the time of the request, to provide the service and keep it secure. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (secure and stable operation).

2.7 Advertising and purchases

The game shows optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland): you decide for every ad whether you watch it, and nothing in the game requires one. Google processes your IP address (and from it an approximate location), interactions with the ad, diagnostic data and device identifiers such as the advertising ID to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. We show personalized ads only with your consent, which you give or refuse in Google's consent dialog after the first start and can change at any time in the settings under "Ad choices". Google's dialog keeps this answer on the device, as Google designs it, so it applies to every game account on that device (unlike the play statistics consent, 2.5). The ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rating PG). The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, section 25(1) TDDDG); for non-personalized ads our legitimate interest in financing the free game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), as far as no consent is needed for them. Google receives no game progress, player names or co-op data from us. More in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

The game currently has no purchases with real money. We will update this policy before any are added.

3. Who processes the data for us

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Remote Config, Multiplayer Services with Relay, Analytics, Engine Diagnostics) as a processor under a data processing agreement. Data may be processed outside the EU, in particular in the USA, based on the EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR) in our data processing agreement with Unity.
  • Google (Google Play) distributes the game as an independent controller under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google (AdMob, with its consent dialog) delivers the rewarded ads as an independent controller (2.7); data may be processed outside the EU, in particular in the USA, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the EU standard contractual clauses.

We do not sell your data.

4. How long we keep data

  • Player account, game progress and friend data: until you delete them (section 5). We do not delete inactive accounts automatically for now; we review this after one year.
  • Play statistics: until you withdraw consent (the game then has Unity delete them), otherwise for Unity Analytics' retention periods.
  • Crash and error reports and technical logs: only as long as Unity keeps them for operation and security.

5. Deleting your data

In the game: MORE › SETTINGS › DELETE MY DATA (you confirm twice). We delete your progress, your profile, your friend links and your play statistics on our servers, then your player account, and the game starts over on your phone as a new player. This cannot be undone. Without the game: see ahnyth.github.io/outbreak/delete.html or write to gfgnp2w@gmail.com with your callsign; we delete within 30 days.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15-21 GDPR) and the right to withdraw a consent at any time with effect for the future. Write to gfgnp2w@gmail.com; we answer within one month. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example where you live or to the one competent for us: the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg, Lautenschlagerstraße 20, 70173 Stuttgart, Germany.

7. Children

The game is meant for players aged 12 and over. You can agree to play statistics only after confirming that you are 16 or older, because in several EU countries, Germany included, consent is valid only from 16 (Art. 8 GDPR). If you believe a child under 12 plays with an account, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Connections between the game and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS). Game data can only be changed through our server rules, not directly by the app.

9. Changes

We update this policy when the game uses data differently and show the date at the top. We announce important changes in the game.
